---
title: "Privacy"
description: "Puck Around collects nothing and transmits nothing — the whole game happens on one device, between the people holding it."
---

**Puck Around does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

The whole game happens on one device, between the people holding it:

- **No data is sent to us.** Puck Around has no server, no account, no
  sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. We receive
  nothing.
- **No network access.** The app makes no network connections of any kind. It
  requests no network-related permissions.
- **What's stored locally.** Only the game's own settings and couch-level
  records — the last match setup, remembered player names and their colors,
  a saved tournament evening, and the arcade's hiscore boards — kept in the
  app's standard local storage so they persist between launches. Deleting the
  app removes them; Settings inside the app can reset the names and boards at
  any time.
- **No tracking.** The app does not track you across apps or websites and
  does not use any device identifiers for advertising.
- **Children.** Because the app collects no data at all, it collects none
  from children either.

*Last updated: 2 September 2026.*
